Dicodex Support

Contact: angasupport@gmail.com

Dicodex turns everyday places into reading hunts for children aged 6–8. It needs an iPhone running iOS 27 with Apple Intelligence enabled, an available Apple model, and camera access for hunts. An internet connection is needed when a parent or carer chooses automatic checking with Apple Processing.

Purchase and download

Dicodex is a paid App Store download. The complete game is included in that upfront price; there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions. To redownload an app bought with the same Apple Account, use the App Store's purchase history or Purchased list. Apple manages billing, refunds and applicable Family Sharing.

Photos and privacy

Use Settings → Privacy & Data to export or delete the family library. There is no Dicodex account. Read the Privacy Policy before allowing optional Apple Processing. When Apple Processing cannot check a photo, the family can review it together.

Get help

Send a message to angasupport@gmail.com. Please describe the iPhone model, iOS version, what you were doing, and any error shown. Do not email a child's photo or personal information unless it is essential to the request.
