Dicodex Support

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Contact: anga-support@angaworks.com

Dicodex turns everyday places into reading hunts for children aged 6–8. It needs an iPhone running iOS 27 with Apple Intelligence enabled, an available Apple model, and camera access for hunts. An internet connection is needed when a parent or carer allows Apple Processing. Scanning is automatic; there is no manual photo-review mode.

The first discovery is your open hand. Setup finishes only after Dicodex checks and saves that photo, so you can confirm scanning works before starting a hunt.

Download and other reading apps

Dicodex is free, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Parents can find an optional recommendation for Anga in Settings → For parents, behind a parental check. Anga is a separate app with optional in-app purchases. Installing it is never required to enjoy Dicodex.

Automatic photo-check limits

Apple sets a daily limit on automatic photo checks. Dicodex cannot show an exact allowance or promise a fixed number. When Apple reports that the limit is near or reached, Dicodex shows a notice. New scanning pauses until Apple's allowance resets. Your confirmed discoveries remain available. A reset time appears when Apple provides it; your local request count is not a remaining balance.

Photos and privacy

Use Settings → Privacy & Data to export or delete the family library. There is no Dicodex account. Read the Privacy Policy before allowing Apple Processing. Only confirmed discoveries are saved. Other photos are discarded when you leave the result; retry when checking is available.

Get help

Send a message to anga-support@angaworks.com. Please describe the iPhone model, iOS version, what you were doing, and any error shown. Do not email a child's photo or personal information unless it is essential to the request.
