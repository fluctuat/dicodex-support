Dicodex Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Publisher: Vincent Debast

Contact: angasupport@gmail.com

Dicodex is a reading game for children aged 6–8 to use with a grown-up. The app asks the family to find everyday objects and photograph them. It does not require an account and has no advertising, third-party analytics, or public sharing.

What the app keeps

Dicodex keeps the family's places, chosen words, progress, favourites and Stash on the iPhone. The Stash may include square photographs with their surroundings and older object cutouts. Camera-file metadata is removed before Dicodex saves a new hunt photo. Dicodex does not add captures to the Photos app. You can review and delete individual Stash items. The adult can export a copy or delete all Dicodex data in Settings → Privacy & Data.

The upfront purchase is handled by Apple's App Store before Dicodex is installed. Dicodex has no in-app purchases or subscriptions and does not receive payment-card details.

Optional Apple Processing

For automatic photo checking, a grown-up must first read and accept a separate Apple Processing disclosure. When this is allowed, Dicodex sends the square photo and the word being hunted to Apple's Private Cloud Compute so Apple's model can identify the visible object and check the word. A place-cover photo can be sent for a suggested place name only after separate consent for that individual photo. You can decline Apple Processing and review discoveries together instead. Adult descriptions used to suggest place words stay on the iPhone and are not sent to Private Cloud Compute.

Dicodex does not run a developer-operated image server. Its local Apple Processing usage counter contains counts and quota state, not photographs, words or labels. Apple's processing and device services are governed by Apple's applicable privacy terms.

Storage, control and retention

The App Store release stores the family library on this iPhone. Apple's normal device backup settings may back up app data; those backups are controlled in iPhone settings. Dicodex keeps its local library until you delete items or use Delete All Dicodex Data. Exporting creates a file in the destination you choose through iOS. Please review the destination before sharing an export.

Camera permission is controlled in iPhone Settings. Dicodex uses the camera for hunts. It uses system speech to read words aloud and does not record your voice. Apple Processing can be turned off in Dicodex's Privacy & Data settings.

Contact and updates

A grown-up can contact angasupport@gmail.com about privacy, access or deletion. We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and show a new date at the top.

The support and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. These pages do not use advertising, analytics or contact forms. If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send; please avoid including a child's photo or personal information unless it is essential to your request.
